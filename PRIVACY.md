# Privacy Policy

**Coptic Prayer**  
Effective date: September 25, 2026

Coptic Prayer is a free Coptic Orthodox prayer application designed to provide the Agpeya, Church readings, the Coptic calendar, Scripture, and optional personal prayer tools. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

## Information stored on your device

Personal Prayer Life information is stored locally on your device. This can include your Personal Prayer Rule selections and reminder times, prayer counters and history, saved intercessors, Father of Confession information you choose to enter, and confession schedule/history.

Coptic Prayer does not ask you to record your sins.

Native prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## Information requested from online services

The app uses internet connections to retrieve content. Church calendar, fasting, readings, Synaxarium, and celebration information is retrieved through Coptic Prayer's read-only Church-data service, which obtains Church data from Coptic.io. Bible chapters may be requested directly from the WEBU Scripture source at eBible.org when you open them.

Like ordinary internet services, these services and hosting infrastructure may receive technical request information such as IP address, request time, device/browser information, and the content requested. Coptic Prayer does not intentionally include your locally stored Prayer Life or confession-planning information in these content requests.

## External links

You may choose to open external Church sources, a parish or priest scheduling page that you entered, a telephone link, or an external calendar service. When you leave Coptic Prayer, the destination service's privacy practices apply.

Calendar links generated by the current Prayer Life experience use discreet wording and do not need to include the name of your Father of Confession or parish.

## Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Coptic Prayer does not require an account for its current prayer experience. The app does not contain advertising and does not intentionally use behavioral advertising or behavioral analytics.

## Browser notifications

The web codebase contains a legacy browser push-notification foundation. Scheduled browser push delivery is currently disabled in the application's configuration. Native prayer reminders use local device notifications.

If browser push is enabled in a future release, this policy will be reviewed and updated before that feature is intentionally offered.

## Voluntary support

Coptic Prayer is free, and prayer content and spiritual tools are not conditioned on payment. A voluntary support option may be offered in the future. Any payment provider used for that feature will have its own privacy practices, and this policy will be updated as necessary before the feature is enabled.

## Data deletion

Because current Personal Prayer Life information is stored locally, clearing the relevant information inside the app or clearing the app's local data removes it from that device. Uninstalling the native app generally removes its local application data according to the operating system's behavior.

## Children

Coptic Prayer is a general prayer and reference application. It is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account system.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
